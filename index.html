About AppliFest 2026

Igniting Tech Innovation at Parul University

Organized by the prestigious Faculty of IT & Computer Science at Parul University, Vadodara, AppliFest 2026 brings together dynamic tech enthusiasts, programmers, designers, and gamers from across institutions under one electric roof.

Under the compilation of Prof. Vivek P. Dave (Head - MCA) and the dedicated organizing committee, AppliFest serves as a launchpad for students to transition from abstract theories into deployable digital products, robust security defenses, and creative visual marvels.

Fair Play First

General Rules & Regulations

To ensure parity, meritocracy, and safety, all registered candidates must adhere strictly to Parul University event standards.

Unfair Means & Zero Tolerance

Any external storage materials, flash drives, unauthorized net-setters, or external devices are strictly prohibited. Interfering with rival teams or competition servers results in immediate disqualification.

Timing & Reporting

Participants must report at least 30 minutes prior to competition kickoff at CV Raman Labs. No extra time will be provided for late arrivals. Every submission must be locked within stipulated hours.

Workstation Monitoring

Judges reserve the absolute right to inspect workstations in-person or via centralized remote surveillance software to ensure genuine work during the competition.

Registered Participants Only

Only verified candidates with official registration receipt badges are permitted to take part in contests. Impersonation will lead to institutional reporting.

Judges' Authority

Judges reserve the right to amend competition instructions or criteria if necessitated by environment. The decision of the evaluation panel shall remain conclusive and final.

Rule Clarifications

In case of doubt regarding permitted software or task interpretation, it is the participant's responsibility to consult coordinators immediately before execution begins.
